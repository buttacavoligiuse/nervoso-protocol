Monetization model

Paid access will use prepaid credits with PayPal receiving payments only. Provider referral/affiliate revenue may also be used where disclosed and permitted. Automated fake clicks, fake referrals and simulated conversions are not part of the model.

Why this exists

Agents should be able to choose compute programmatically instead of scraping pricing pages or relying on a human to compare providers. The router focuses on normalized prices, workload fit and machine-readable responses.

Managed inference vs raw GPU rental

The cheapest hourly GPU is not always the cheapest way to serve a model. Raw rental gives maximum control, but you also pay for setup, idle time, storage and retries. Managed endpoints can remove some of those costs, especially when traffic is uneven.

FactorRaw GPU rentalManaged inference
ControlHighLower, provider-managed
Idle costUsually paid while instance is runningCan be lower with scale-to-zero
Setup/opsYou manage container, serving stack and reliabilityProvider manages serving layer
Best fitTraining, custom stacks, sustained utilizationProduction inference, bursty traffic, simpler ops

Current example

Novita currently lists dedicated endpoint pricing of $1.99/GPU-h for H100 SXM and $2.99/GPU-h for H200 SXM, billed per second on running replicas, with no charge when scaled to zero or stopped. Those rates should not be compared blindly with raw pods because the product and operational burden differ.

Choose raw rental if

  • you need root/container control;
  • you are training or running custom systems;
  • utilization will stay high enough to justify the instance.

Choose managed inference if

  • traffic is bursty or unpredictable;
  • you want scale-to-zero;
  • serving operations are more expensive to you than provider markup.

H100 vs H200: compare workload economics, not just GPU names

H100 and H200 are not interchangeable purely on hourly price. H200 offers 141 GB VRAM, while H100 variants commonly provide 80 GB. If a model or batch fits comfortably on H100, paying more for H200 can be unnecessary; if extra memory avoids sharding, retries or multi-GPU complexity, H200 can be cheaper overall.

Rule of thumb

  • Choose H100 when 80 GB is enough and raw hourly cost dominates.
  • Choose H200 when 141 GB avoids model splitting or extra GPUs.
  • Choose managed inference when idle time and infrastructure work would otherwise dominate the bill.
